# ZPTech Homepage Hero Video Brief

## Goal
Create a real-life, premium 15–20 second seamless hero loop for ZPTech.

## Story
A subtle visual bridge between science and holistic living. The viewer should feel that ZPTech belongs in a world of precision, vitality, nature and intentional living without ever seeing a product.

## Suggested sequence
0–4s: Dawn landscape, soft natural light, slow camera movement.
4–8s: Real active adult moving outdoors, hiking, walking or light mobility work.
8–12s: Quiet breath / meditation / eyes closed / hands resting, shot naturally.
12–16s: Wider nature shot with a person moving through the frame.
16–20s: Return to a composition similar to the opening frame so the loop is visually soft.

## Avoid
- Product bottles, powders, capsules or packaging
- Fake laboratory props that feel staged
- Floating particles, glowing energy, synthetic light beams
- Unreal skin, faces, hands or body motion
- Overly dramatic wellness clichés
- Obvious AI-generated footage

## Technical delivery
- 16:9
- 1920×1080 or 2560×1440 master
- H.264 MP4
- Silent
- 24–30 fps
- 15–20 seconds
- Web-optimized bitrate
- Poster JPG/WebP
- Optional WebM/AV1 alternative if your CDN pipeline supports it
